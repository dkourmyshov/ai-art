## Current Piece
MEMBRANE — Gray-Scott Reaction-Diffusion (2026-06-28)
WebGL generative art. Two chemical species react and diffuse across a 2D plane,
producing emergent organic patterns inspired by Turing's 1952 morphogenesis paper.

## Technical Stack
- Pure WebGL1 + OES_texture_float (no external dependencies, ~200 lines)
- Ping-pong framebuffer technique for GPU-accelerated PDE solving
- 9-point discrete Laplacian (weights: orthogonal=0.2, diagonal=0.05, center=-1.0)
- 5 simulation steps per animation frame
- Sim resolution: capped at 640px per longest edge (performance)
- Linear upscaling from sim to screen when OES_texture_float_linear available
- IQ cosine palette: 0.5 + 0.5*cos(2pi*(vec3(0,.33,.67) + t)) for coloring

## Pattern Modes (auto-cycle every ~30s, smooth parameter interpolation)
- mitosis:  f=0.0545, k=0.062  (dividing droplets)
- coral:    f=0.0545, k=0.0595 (branching coral-like forms)
- spirals:  f=0.034,  k=0.063  (rotating wave spirals)
- maze:     f=0.030,  k=0.057  (labyrinthine channels)
- spots:    f=0.039,  k=0.058  (stable dot lattice)
- stripes:  f=0.046,  k=0.066  (banded / striped)

## Interaction
- Mouse/touch over canvas: seeds V chemical (U=0.5, V=0.9) at cursor position
- Window resize: reinitializes simulation with fresh random seeds
